3. Scope

Build

  • Mobile plans, data add-ons, wireless + fibre broadband
  • Grants, rollover, Endless throttle vs capped hard-stop
  • $10 broadband discount when mobile is $27+
  • 12-month vs open term (modem $0 vs $199, $10 shipping)
  • 52-week prepaid lock (no plan change)
  • Auto-renew, wallet, payment fail / lapse
  • Synthetic data usage so grants move

Do not build

  • CPQ, Channel360, Workflow360, SelfCare360
  • SIM activation, number port, Spark network / OCS
  • Retail partner commissions
  • Everyday Rewards points
  • Real Skinny customer data
  • One NZ / Matrixx / BRM story

Rule DISC-HH-10: $10 off broadband when the account holds an active mobile plan priced $27 or above. Modem MDM-TERM $0 on 12-month / MDM-OPEN $199 open term. Shipping SHIP-MDM $10. New wireless on 12-month: first 60 days plan charge $0.

Rules that must actually run, not just be described

  • Rollover on $13 / $17 / $22 / $27 included data and minutes only. Rolls if that eligible plan auto-renews. Valid 12 months. Add-ons do not roll.
  • Capped: data stops at zero until an add-on or a new plan.
  • Endless: after the max-speed bucket, stay active at 1.2 Mbps — do not suspend.
  • 52-week: plan change blocked. Wallet credit cannot pay the yearly pack.
  • Lapse: failed auto-renew → standard rates or suspend. Show it once.

5. Demo paths

Four must-show subscribers. Present from Subscriber360; cut to Pricing Studio or the invoice only to prove the rule. Optional extras (family link, 36-month handset) only if time remains.

A · Aria Rangi — rollover + add-on

  1. Open Aria on MOB-27-28, Set & Forget, invoice $27.
  2. Show this cycle’s 4 GB included grant and 1.2 GB rolled from last cycle as separate balances.
  3. Usage already at 4.8 GB — included gone, rollover almost gone.
  4. Buy ADD-DATA-1GB-28 live. Grant goes up / data unblocks.

B · Ben Chen — Endless throttle

  1. Open Ben on MOB-40-28. Max-speed bucket 10 GB, usage 10.4 GB.
  2. Subscriber still active. Policy shows 1.2 Mbps — not barred, not suspended.
  3. Optional: add 2 GB and show max-speed restored.

C · Chloe Patel — 52-week lock

  1. Chloe bought MOB-296-52 60 days ago. Upfront $296. Grants refreshed twice (28-day cadence).
  2. Wallet shows $50 credit that could not have paid this pack.
  3. Attempt change to MOB-40-28 — blocked.

D · Dylan Fa'umuina — household bundle

  1. Same customer: MOB-27-28 + wireless 60 GB on a 12-month term.
  2. Broadband period charge is $35, not $45.
  3. First invoice: modem $0, shipping $10, 60-day wireless promo visible.
  4. Rehearsal toggle: remove the $27 mobile and broadband returns to $45.

6. Ready when

A presenter who did not configure the tenant can walk paths A–D without opening a generic US catalog.

  • Aria’s rolled GB is visibly distinct from this cycle’s 4 GB.
  • Ben is throttled, not barred.
  • Chloe cannot change plan.
  • Dylan’s broadband is $35 with $27 mobile, $45 without it.
  • Invoices show NZD and GST.
  • No One NZ, Kordia, Class, or “Sam Smith” names on screen.
